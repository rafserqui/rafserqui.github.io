Hello, I am Rafael Serrano-Quintero, an Assistant Professor at the [**Department of Economic Theory**](https://www.ub.edu/school-economics/) at the [**University of Barcelona**](https://www.ub.edu/web/portal/en/).

I got my PhD from the [**Department of Economics (FAE)**](https://dfae.ua.es/en/home.html) at the University of Alicante in 2021.

My research interests are macroeconomics, economic growth, development, and structural change. I am also working on spatial models of economic development.
